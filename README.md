# PlanQuote — 100% Offline Browser PDF Blueprint Takeoff & Quote Generator

A lightweight, zero-signup, client-side floor plan takeoff and client quote PDF generator built for small flooring, landscaping, painting, roofing, and remodeling contractors.

- **Live Free Demo:** https://frank20190926.github.io/planquote/
- **Offline Pro Zip ($29 one-time, zero watermark + custom logo + saved pricebooks):** https://suiyiverse0.gumroad.com/l/planquote

## Why

Small contractors are frequently pushed into $50–$200/month cloud subscriptions just to measure room square footage, linear feet, and generate a clean estimate from a client's PDF plan. PlanQuote runs 100% locally in the browser with bundled PDF.js — your drawings never leave your device.

## Key Features

- Drop any multi-page PDF or PNG/JPG floor plan (loads a sample residential plan by default so you can test in 5 seconds)
- 2-click scale calibration with instant Imperial (`ft / sq ft`) and Metric (`m / m²`) switching
- Polygon area + perimeter, linear path, and pin count measurements
- 5 editable trade presets with waste % and material/labor rates (Flooring & Tile, Interior Painting, Landscaping & Decking, Roofing & Pergola, General Remodel)
- Single-page print-ready client quote PDF (includes annotated plan snapshot) + clean Excel CSV export
